# CrewCommute

Airline Crew Commuter Planning Tool

Live Demo:
https://crew-commute-demo.streamlit.app

CrewCommute helps airline crew members evaluate commuting reliability
and non-revenue travel options when traveling to their assigned base.
---

## Overview

CrewCommute is a concept platform designed to help airline pilots and flight attendants manage one of the most stressful parts of aviation life: commuting to work.

Many airline crew members live in cities different from their assigned base. This often requires them to fly standby or non-revenue on other flights in order to report for duty. Uncertainty around seat availability, delays, and flight loads can create significant operational stress.

CrewCommute explores how data analytics and predictive tools could help airline crew make smarter commuting decisions.

---

## Problem

Airline crew commuting is often unpredictable and risky.

Common challenges include:

- Uncertain standby seat availability
- Limited visibility into flight loads
- Last-minute schedule changes
- Delays or cancellations affecting commute plans
- Lack of tools designed specifically for crew commuting logistics

Missing a commute can result in missed assignments, disciplinary risk, and operational disruption.

---

## Concept

CrewCommute aims to provide a **decision-support platform for airline crew commuters**.

The system would analyze available flights, historical load patterns, and operational variables to help crew members choose the safest commuting options.

Instead of guessing which flight to take, CrewCommute would provide **data-driven commuting recommendations.**
